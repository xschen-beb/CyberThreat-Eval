# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: BlackSuit (Royal) Ransomware 

 Root cause: The root cause includes multiple vectors: phishing emails with malicious attachments or links, RDP compromise, vulnerable public-facing applications, and brokered access via harvested VPN credentials. Actors used legitimate software and open-source tools like Cobalt Strike, SystemBC, Rubeus, and SharpHound for command and control, persistence, lateral movement, and data exfiltration. *The threat actor's command and control traffic was proxied through CloudFlare to conceal their Cobalt Strike server* (https://thedfirreport.com/2024/08/26/blacksuit-ransomware/). *They also exploited AnyDesk and MobaXterm tools for remote access and persistence* (https://socradar.io/dark-web-profile-blacksuit-ransomware/). *The group also used Gootloader malware and penetration testing tools like Ursnif/Gozi* (https://www.hipaajournal.com/cisa-fbi-royal-blacksuit-ransomware/). *BlackSuit actors disable antivirus software and extract large amounts of data before ultimately deploying the ransomware* (https://sprysquared.com/cisa-and-fbi-release-update-to-advisory-royal-ransomware-actors-rebrand-ad-blacksuit-august-7-2024/). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: BlackSuit (formerly known as Royal) Ransomware 

 Organization/industry/location: Multiple sectors and industries across the US, including the Communications Sector, Critical Manufacturing Sector, and Healthcare and Public Health Sector. *BlackSuit has targeted over 350 victims globally* (https://socradar.io/dark-web-profile-blacksuit-ransomware/). *Notable victims include the City of Dallas, TX* (https://www.hipaajournal.com/cisa-fbi-royal-blacksuit-ransomware/). *Octapharma, a leading blood plasma provider, was attacked in April 2024, disrupting operations across 35 states in the U.S.* (https://sprysquared.com/cisa-and-fbi-release-update-to-advisory-royal-ransomware-actors-rebrand-ad-blacksuit-august-7-2024/). *Kershaw County School District and Henry County School system were also targeted* (https://sprysquared.com/cisa-and-fbi-release-update-to-advisory-royal-ransomware-actors-rebrand-ad-blacksuit-august-7-2024/). 

 Start date – End date: The advisory does not provide specific start and end dates for the attacks but mentions activities observed from September 2022 onwards, with recent updates as of August 2024. *BlackSuit first emerged in May 2023* (https://socradar.io/dark-web-profile-blacksuit-ransomware/).

 Impact: Over $500 million USD in ransom demanded, with individual ransom demands ranging from $1 million to $10 million USD. The largest individual ransom demand was $60 million. Financial and operational impacts include data encryption, exfiltration, and potential public release of sensitive data. *BlackSuit also leveraged double extortion tactics* (https://www.hipaajournal.com/cisa-fbi-royal-blacksuit-ransomware/). *BlackSuit actors have shown a readiness to negotiate ransom amounts* (https://sprysquared.com/cisa-and-fbi-release-update-to-advisory-royal-ransomware-actors-rebrand-ad-blacksuit-august-7-2024/). 

 Mitigation: ['Implement a recovery plan to maintain and retain multiple copies of sensitive data in a secure, segmented location.', 'Require compliance with NIST standards for password policies, including longer passwords, password managers, and account lockouts.', 'Keep all operating systems, software, and firmware up to date, prioritizing the patching of known vulnerabilities.', 'Require multi-factor authentication (MFA) for administrator accounts and critical services.', 'Segment networks to prevent lateral movement.', 'Use network monitoring tools to detect abnormal activity and potential ransomware traversal.', 'Regularly update and enable real-time detection for antivirus software on all hosts.', 'Implement secure logging collection and storage practices.', 'Review domain controllers, servers, and workstations for new or unrecognized accounts.', 'Audit user accounts with administrative privileges and configure access controls according to the principle of least privilege.', 'Disable unused ports and enforce email security policies.', 'Disable macros by default and consider adding email banners for external emails.', 'Implement time-based access for admin-level accounts and disable command-line scripting activities.', 'Maintain offline backups of data and ensure backups are encrypted and immutable.', '*Consider HC3 alerts for healthcare-related threats* (https://www.hipaajournal.com/cisa-fbi-royal-blacksuit-ransomware/).', '*Review updated advisory and implement suggested measures* (https://sprysquared.com/cisa-and-fbi-release-update-to-advisory-royal-ransomware-actors-rebrand-ad-blacksuit-august-7-2024/).']
