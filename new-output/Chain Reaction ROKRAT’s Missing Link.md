 Incident: Chain Reaction: ROKRAT’s Missing Link 

 Root cause: The root cause behind the incident is the use of malicious LNK files to deliver the ROKRAT malware. These LNK files are often disguised as legitimate documents and execute PowerShell scripts to initiate multi-stage infection chains, ultimately leading to the deployment of the ROKRAT malware. 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: The attack was carried out by the North Korean threat actor commonly referred to as APT37, Inky Squid, RedEyes, Reaper, or ScarCruft, which also deploys M2RAT, Konni RAT, Chinotto, and GOLDBACKDOOR *The changes* (https://cirt.gy/article/al2023_36-rokrat-malware-deployed-via-lnk-file-infection-(16th-may-2023)/). 

 Organization/industry/location: The targets were primarily South Korean individuals and organizations, including government sectors, journalists, activists, and North Korean defectors. 

 Start date – End date: The activity mentioned in the blog spans from July 2022 to April 2023. 

 MITRE TTPs: ['T1204.002: User Execution: Malicious File', 'T1059.001: Command and Scripting Interpreter: PowerShell', 'T1566.001: Phishing: Spear Phishing Attachment', 'T1203: Exploitation for Client Execution', 'T1071.001: Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols', '*CVE-2013-0808: Exploitation of HWP documents* (https://blog.talosintelligence.com/introducing-rokrat/)'] 

 Impact: The exact number of devices and financial losses are not specified in the document. However, the impact includes the deployment of ROKRAT malware, exfiltration of data, and potential further exploitation of compromised systems.
