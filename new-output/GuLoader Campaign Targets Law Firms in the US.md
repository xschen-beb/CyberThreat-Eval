 Incident: GuLoader Campaign Targets Law Firms in the US 

 Root cause: The root cause behind the incident is the successful execution of phishing campaigns that trick users into downloading malicious payloads via legitimate hosting services like GitHub. Specifically, the use of malicious PDFs with embedded links that lead to the download of GuLoader VBScript. 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: Unknown threat actor(s) utilizing GuLoader (also known as Cloudeye) as part of their malware distribution campaign. 

 Organization/industry/location: Law firms, healthcare, and investment firms in the United States were targeted. 

 Start date – End date: The campaign has been active since April 2023.
