# Kimsuky Evolves Reconnaissance Capabilities in New Global Campaign

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Kimsuky ReconShark Campaign 

 Root cause: The root cause includes spear-phishing emails targeting specific individuals, using OneDrive links leading to document downloads with malicious macros. Additionally, Kimsuky employed spoofed URLs and impersonation tactics to steal credentials *The changes* (https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/kimsuky-expands-social-engineering/). *ReconShark malware* (https://dmarcreport.com/blog/kimsuky-spear-phishing-worldwide-2023-social-phishing-threat-phishing-html-doubles/) is deployed through these spear-phishing campaigns. 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: North Korean state-sponsored APT group Kimsuky, also known as APT43, ARCHIPELAGO, Black Banshee, Emerald Sleet, Nickel Kimball, and Velvet Chollima *The changes* (https://thehackernews.com/2023/05/n-korean-kimsuky-hackers-using-new.html). 

 Organization/industry/location: Targeted organizations across North America, Asia, and Europe, including think tanks, research universities, government entities, and specifically the Korea Risk Group (KRG) and U.S. National Security Think Tanks *The changes* (https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/new-babyshark-malware-targets-u-s-national-security-think-tanks/). Kimsuky also targeted experts in North Korean affairs *The changes* (https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/kimsuky-expands-social-engineering/). 

 Start date – End date: Ongoing as of May 4, 2023 

 MITRE TTPs: ['T1566.002: Phishing: Spearphishing Link', 'T1204: User Execution', 'T1059.005: Command and Scripting Interpreter: Visual Basic', 'T1083: File and Directory Discovery', 'T1047: Windows Management Instrumentation', 'T1071.001: Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols', 'T1105: Ingress Tool Transfer', 'T1056: Input Capture', 'T1059.007: Command and Scripting Interpreter: JavaScript'] 

 Impact: The exact number of records leaked or the financial losses is not specified. However, the impact involves exfiltration of sensitive information and potential subsequent precision attacks, including attacks on North Korea denuclearization conference *The changes* (https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/new-babyshark-malware-targets-u-s-national-security-think-tanks/). ReconShark can exfiltrate *detection mechanisms and hardware info* (https://dmarcreport.com/blog/kimsuky-spear-phishing-worldwide-2023-social-phishing-threat-phishing-html-doubles/). Kimsuky aimed to gain strategic intelligence by stealing Google and subscription credentials *The changes* (https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/kimsuky-expands-social-engineering/).
