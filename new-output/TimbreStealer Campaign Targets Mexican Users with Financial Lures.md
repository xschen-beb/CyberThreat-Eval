# TimbreStealer Campaign Targets Mexican Users with Financial Lures

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: TimbreStealer Campaign Targets Mexican IT Users with Financial Lures 

 Root cause: The root cause behind the incident is the exploitation of web servers to host and distribute the TimbreStealer malware through phishing campaigns. The campaign uses compromised websites and leverages geofenced WebDAV servers to distribute the payload. The phishing campaign uses Mexico's digital tax receipt standard called *Comprobante Fiscal Digital por Internet (CDFI)* (https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/timbrestealer-malware-targets/). *The campaign employs techniques such as custom loaders, direct system calls, and the Heaven’s Gate technique to evade detection* (https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tax-themed-phishing-scam-propagating-timbrestealer-malware-dan-duran-8ls5c). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: The threat actor is not explicitly named but is associated with the same TTPs as the group distributing the Mispadu banking trojan. 

 Organization/industry/location: The campaign targets IT users in Mexico, particularly focusing on financial themes related to Mexican tax-related topics and *also targets the manufacturing and transportation sectors* (https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tax-themed-phishing-scam-propagating-timbrestealer-malware-dan-duran-8ls5c). 

 Start date – End date: The campaign began in at least November 2023 and is ongoing as of February 2024.
