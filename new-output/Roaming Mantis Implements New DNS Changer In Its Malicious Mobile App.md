# Roaming Mantis Implements New DNS Changer In Its Malicious Mobile App

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Roaming Mantis DNS Changer in Malicious Mobile App 

 Root cause: The Roaming Mantis group exploits vulnerable Wi-Fi routers via a DNS changer function in their malicious Android malware, Wroba.o/Agent.eq, to hijack DNS settings on Wi-Fi routers. This enables redirection of traffic to malicious landing pages and installation of *Trojanized applications facebook.apk and chrome.apk* (https://securelist.com/roaming-mantis-uses-dns-hijacking-to-infect-android-smartphones/85178/). They also deploy the *XLoader payload* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/roaming-mantis-hits-android-and-ios-users-in-malware-phishing-attacks/) on Android devices and use base64 encoded Imgur profile destinations to retrieve C2 configurations. The campaign employs *smishing tactics* (https://www.phishprotection.com/cybersecurity/infamous-smishing-campaign-roaming-mantis-hits-users-france) to trick users into clicking malicious URLs. The malware uses *kuronekoyamato.apk* (https://securelist.com/roaming-mantis-reaches-europe/105596/) to deliver its payload. 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: Roaming Mantis (a.k.a Shaoye) 

 Organization/industry/location: Targets include users in South Korea, Japan, France, Germany, the United States, Taiwan, Turkey, and other regions. *French users* (https://www.phishprotection.com/cybersecurity/infamous-smishing-campaign-roaming-mantis-hits-users-france) are currently being specifically targeted. 

 Start date – End date: Ongoing since at least 2018 with significant activity in 2022. 

 MITRE TTPs: ['T1071.001: Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols', 'T1190: Exploit Public-Facing Application', 'T1203: Exploitation for Client Execution', 'T1046: Network Service Scanning'] 

 Impact: Numerous infected devices and compromised Wi-Fi routers in targeted regions. Specific download statistics include 24,645 APK downloads in Japan, 7,354 in Austria, and 7,246 in France. The malware's malicious payload, *test.dex* (https://securelist.com/roaming-mantis-uses-dns-hijacking-to-infect-android-smartphones/85178/), significantly impacts users. The campaign also involves *phishing page for Apple credentials* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/roaming-mantis-hits-android-and-ios-users-in-malware-phishing-attacks/). Additionally, *SEKOIA cybersecurity firm* (https://www.phishprotection.com/cybersecurity/infamous-smishing-campaign-roaming-mantis-hits-users-france) reports significant activity targeting *French users*. The malware now includes *backdoor commands* (https://securelist.com/roaming-mantis-reaches-europe/105596/) to steal photos and galleries from infected devices. 

 Mitigation: ['Change default credentials on Wi-Fi routers to strong, unique passwords.', 'Regularly update router firmware to patch known vulnerabilities.', 'Use security solutions capable of detecting and mitigating threats like HEUR:Trojan-Dropper.AndroidOS.Wroba.o or HEUR:Trojan-Dropper.AndroidOS.Agent.eq.', 'Implement network segmentation to isolate critical systems from insecure devices.', 'Educate users on the risks of connecting to public Wi-Fi networks and encourage the use of VPNs.']
