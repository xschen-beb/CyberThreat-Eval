# Chinese Entanglement  DLL Hijacking in the Asian Gambling Sector

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Chinese Entanglement | DLL Hijacking in the Asian Gambling Sector 

 Root cause: The incident involved vulnerable executables (Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft Edge, McAfee VirusScan) prone to DLL hijacking, aiding the deployment of Cobalt Strike beacons. The compromise of a code-signing certificate issued to PMG PTE LTD facilitated the attack. *The deployment of the HUI Loader to load remote access trojans and ransomware like LockFile and AtomSilo played a crucial role* (https://www.secureworks.com/research/bronze-starlight-ransomware-operations-use-hui-loader). *The hackers also exploited a stolen Ivacy VPN certificate to bypass security measures* (https://blog.koddos.net/chinese-hacker-group-targets-southeast-asian-gambling-industry-using-stolen-ivacy-vpn-certificate/). *Earth Freybug utilized UNAPIMON malware for stealthy operations* (https://thehackernews.com/search/label/DLL%20hijacking). *Additionally, BluetoothDiagnosticUtil.dll was used to launch the final payload* (https://thehackernews.com/search/label/DLL%20hijacking). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: The China-aligned BRONZE STARLIGHT group, also known as DEV-0401 or SLIME34, executed the attack, with potential involvement of interconnected Chinese APT groups. *BRONZE RIVERSIDE also appears to be linked to similar activities* (https://www.secureworks.com/research/bronze-starlight-ransomware-operations-use-hui-loader). *The campaign is part of Operation ChattyGoblin* (https://blog.koddos.net/chinese-hacker-group-targets-southeast-asian-gambling-industry-using-stolen-ivacy-vpn-certificate/). *Earth Freybug, a subset of APT41, was also active* (https://thehackernews.com/search/label/DLL%20hijacking). 

 Organization/industry/location: Targeted the gambling sector within Southeast Asia, specifically companies in the Philippines. 

 Start date – End date: Activities observed in March 2023. *The issue dates back to May 29, 2023* (https://blog.koddos.net/chinese-hacker-group-targets-southeast-asian-gambling-industry-using-stolen-ivacy-vpn-certificate/).
