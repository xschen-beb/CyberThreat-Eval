# RecordBreaker Stealer Distributed via Hacked YouTube Accounts

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 ### Incident: RecordBreaker Stealer Distributed via Hacked YouTube Accounts: {"**Root cause:** The incident stemmed from compromised YouTube accounts with significant follower bases. These hacked accounts were used to distribute the RecordBreaker Stealer malware through videos promoting fake software cracks and installers. The malware, a successor to Raccoon Stealer and a full rewrite in C/C++ *The changes* (https://malpedia.caad.fkie.fraunhofer.de/details/win.recordbreaker), is similar to other info-stealers like CryptBot, RedLine, and Vidar *The changes* (https://asec.ahnlab.com/en/35981/). **Threat Actor/group/campaign:** The specific threat actor remains unidentified, but it's assumed to be a group or individual capable of compromising YouTube accounts and distributing malware, akin to those previously linked with Raccoon Stealer *The changes* (https://asec.ahnlab.com/en/35981/). **Organization/industry/location:** The targeted victims are YouTube users globally, especially those searching for software cracks or keygens. **Start date – End date:** The exact start and end dates are unspecified, but activity was observed around April 27, 2023. **MITRE TTPs:** - T1071.001: Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols - T1566.002: Phishing: Spearphishing Link - T1078: Valid Accounts - T1189: Drive-by Compromise - T1105: Ingress Tool Transfer **Impact:** Potentially millions of users exposed to malware via popular YouTube channels.
