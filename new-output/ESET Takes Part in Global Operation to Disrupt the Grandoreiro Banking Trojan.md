# ESET Takes Part in Global Operation to Disrupt the Grandoreiro Banking Trojan

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Grandoreiro Banking Trojan Disruption 

 Root cause: The primary vulnerability exploited was the use of cloud providers like Azure and AWS to host C&C servers, facilitating the Grandoreiro banking trojan operations. Additionally, the malware's Domain Generation Algorithm (DGA) and the abuse of No-IP’s Dynamic DNS service enabled frequent changes in domain and IP addresses, complicating detection and takedown efforts. Notably, Grandoreiro exhibits rapid development and a modular architecture *rapid development and a modular architecture* (https://www.welivesecurity.com/2020/04/28/grandoreiro-how-engorged-can-exe-get/). *It uses advanced evasion techniques* (https://any.run/malware-trends/grandoreiro#:~:text=Grandoreiro%20malware%20technical%20details,-The%20main%20functionality&text=Some%20of%20the%20key%20capabilities,the%20main%20banking%20trojan%20payload.). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: Grandoreiro Operators (likely a single group or closely cooperating groups) 

 Organization/industry/location: Targets were primarily in Brazil, Mexico, Spain, and Peru *Peru* (https://www.welivesecurity.com/2020/04/28/grandoreiro-how-engorged-can-exe-get/). The disruption was led by the Federal Police of Brazil with technical assistance from ESET and cooperation from Interpol, the Spanish National Police, and Caixa Bank *Interpol and the Spanish National Police* (https://www.gov.br/pf/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2024/01/pf-combate-organizacao-criminosa-que-praticava-fraudes-bancarias-eletronicas-contra-vitimas-no-exterior). *Caixa Bank* (https://cert.europa.eu/publications/threat-intelligence/cb24-02/). 

 Start date – End date: Grandoreiro has been active since at least 2017, with consistent tracking and analysis by ESET since then. The disruption operation was reported on January 30, 2024. *Grandoreiro was first observed in 2016 and tracked by IBM X-Force since March 2024* (https://any.run/malware-trends/grandoreiro#:~:text=Grandoreiro%20malware%20technical%20details,-The%20main%20functionality&text=Some%20of%20the%20key%20capabilities,the%20main%20banking%20trojan%20payload.) *IBM X-Force* (https://cyberinsider.com/grandoreiro-banking-trojan-surges-globally-following-police-disruption/).

 Impact: The average number of daily victims was 563, with 551 unique victims, and an average of 114 new unique victims per day. The operation saw multiple arrests and the disruption of the Grandoreiro operation, which had illicitly moved at least 3.6 million euros since 2019 *3.6 million euros* (https://www.gov.br/pf/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/2024/01/pf-combate-organizacao-criminosa-que-praticava-fraudes-bancarias-eletronicas-contra-vitimas-no-exterior). Additionally, the trojan stole Google Chrome credentials and abused the fear around COVID-19 through spam *Google Chrome credentials and COVID-19 spam* (https://www.welivesecurity.com/2020/04/28/grandoreiro-how-engorged-can-exe-get/). *In March 2024, it launched a massive attack targeting over 1,500 banks' applications worldwide* (https://any.run/malware-trends/grandoreiro#:~:text=Grandoreiro%20malware%20technical%20details,-The%20main%20functionality&text=Some%20of%20the%20key%20capabilities,the%20main%20banking%20trojan%20payload.). *IBM X-Force reported extensive phishing campaigns* (https://cyberinsider.com/grandoreiro-banking-trojan-surges-globally-following-police-disruption/). *The operation caused around 120.000.000 US dollars in losses* (https://cert.europa.eu/publications/threat-intelligence/cb24-02/).
