# Chinese Threat Actors Targeting Europe in SmugX Campaign

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: SmugX Campaign Targeting Europe and Asia Pacific 

 Root cause: The root cause includes the use of HTML Smuggling to deliver malicious payloads, leveraging DLL sideloading techniques to deploy PlugX malware, and the use of DLL Loaders *The changes* (https://blogs.blackberry.com/en/2022/12/mustang-panda-uses-the-russian-ukrainian-war-to-attack-europe-and-asia-pacific-targets). Misconfiguration or lack of security controls allowing the execution of these malicious HTML files and PowerShell scripts also contributed. 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: RedDelta, Mustang Panda (also known as HoneyMyte, Bronze President), Chinese state-sponsored hackers *The changes* (https://blog.koddos.net/check-point-researchers-disclose-a-hacking-campaign-targeting-key-european-entities/) 

 Organization/industry/location: Governmental entities in Europe, especially targeting Foreign Affairs ministries and embassies in Eastern Europe, with additional targets in Asia Pacific *The changes* (https://blogs.blackberry.com/en/2022/12/mustang-panda-uses-the-russian-ukrainian-war-to-attack-europe-and-asia-pacific-targets). Countries affected include the UK, France, Sweden, Ukraine, Czech, Hungary, and Slovakia *The changes* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-target-european-government-entities-in-smugx-campaign/). 

 Start date – End date: Since at least December 2022, ongoing.

 Impact: The exact number of records leaked or the financial losses is not specified, but the campaign targeted sensitive governmental entities which could lead to significant political and security impacts.

 Additional Notes: Check Point Research (CPR) analysts have been tracking the SmugX campaign for the last two months *The changes* (https://www.darkreading.com/endpoint-security/group-tied-to-china-s-mustang-panda-targets-european-governments-with-smugx). The campaign uses HTML documents with diplomatic-related content to target victims, including a letter from the Serbian embassy in Budapest, an invitation to a diplomatic conference by the Hungarian foreign ministry *The changes* (https://blog.koddos.net/check-point-researchers-disclose-a-hacking-campaign-targeting-key-european-entities/), and a document on the Swedish Presidency of the EU Council *The changes* (https://www.darkreading.com/endpoint-security/group-tied-to-china-s-mustang-panda-targets-european-governments-with-smugx). The researchers based their analysis on samples posted on VirusTotal malware repository *The changes* (https://blog.koddos.net/check-point-researchers-disclose-a-hacking-campaign-targeting-key-european-entities/). The campaign also utilized several .docx and .pdf files containing diplomatic content. The malware was previously used in USB drive-based hacking campaigns across Ghana, Mongolia, and other regions *The changes* (https://blog.koddos.net/check-point-researchers-disclose-a-hacking-campaign-targeting-key-european-entities/).
