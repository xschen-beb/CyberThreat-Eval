# Vice Society Ransomware Group Targets Manufacturing Companies

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Vice Society Ransomware Group Targets Manufacturing Companies and Education Sector *The changes* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa22-249a-0). 

 Root Cause: The incident was primarily caused by the exploitation of vulnerable public-facing websites and compromised Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) credentials. This allowed the attackers to gain initial access and further leverage tools like Cobalt Strike and Mimikatz for lateral movement and credential harvesting. Additionally, the group used a custom encryptor named PolyVice, which combines asymmetric encryption with the NTRUEncrypt algorithm and symmetric encryption with the ChaCha20-Poly1305 algorithm *The changes* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/vice-society-ransomware-gang-switches-to-new-custom-encryptor/). The attackers also used phishing and spear phishing emails, PrintNightmare exploitation (CVE-2021-34527 and CVE-2021-1675) *The changes* (https://www.sentinelone.com/anthology/vice-society/), and deployed versions of Hello Kitty/Five Hands and Zeppelin ransomware *The changes* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa22-249a-0). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: Vice Society Ransomware Group, which emerged in early to mid-2021 *The changes* (https://www.sentinelone.com/anthology/vice-society/). 

 Organization/industry/location: Manufacturing companies in Brazil, Argentina, Switzerland, and Israel were among the targets. Vice Society also targets ESXi or heavily virtualized environments *The changes* (https://www.sentinelone.com/anthology/vice-society/). Additionally, the education sector, especially K-12 institutions, has been disproportionately targeted *The changes* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa22-249a-0). 

 Start date – End date: The attacks were observed from late 2022 to early 2023, with significant activity noted between October 28, 2022, and January 2023.
