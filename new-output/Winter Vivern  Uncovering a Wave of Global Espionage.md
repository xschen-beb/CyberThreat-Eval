# Winter Vivern  Uncovering a Wave of Global Espionage

# Enriched Doc (enrihcments marked with *content*(link)): 
 Incident: Winter Vivern Espionage Campaign 

 Root cause: The root cause includes phishing websites, credential phishing, and deployment of malicious documents. Attackers leveraged authentic government documents and crafted phishing webpages mimicking legitimate sites for malicious downloads. *Additionally, they utilized XLM 4.0 macros in Lithuanian-language documents* (https://www.domaintools.com/resources/blog/winter-vivern-a-look-at-re-crafted-government-maldocs/). *Furthermore, the Excel documents revealed that the macros were potentially created using SharpShooter* (https://lab52.io/blog/winter-vivern-all-summer/). The APT, identified as TA473, exploited a Zimbra software vulnerability (CVE-2022-27926) *to execute arbitrary JavaScript or HTML code* (https://www.avertium.com/resources/threat-reports/apt-winter-vivern-resurfaces). They used tools such as Acunetix for scanning unpatched webmail portals to deliver bespoke JavaScript payloads *targeting RoundCube webmail in some cases* (https://www.proofpoint.com/us/blog/threat-insight/exploitation-dish-best-served-cold-winter-vivern-uses-known-zimbra-vulnerability). *The CERT-UA#5909 alert highlighted a fake webpage impersonating the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, luring users into downloading the malicious Protector.bat file which launches PowerShell scripts, including a recursive search algorithm for files and screen capturing* (https://socprime.com/blog/uac-0114-group-aka-winter-vivern-attack-detection-hackers-launch-malicious-phishing-campaigns-targeting-government-entities-of-ukraine-and-poland/). 

 Threat Actor/group/campaign: Winter Vivern Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) 

 Organization/industry/location: The campaign targeted government organizations in Lithuania, India, Vatican, Slovakia, Poland, Ukraine, Italy, and private businesses including telecommunications supporting Ukraine. *Specific targeted entities included the Segreteria di Stato in Vatican City* (https://lab52.io/blog/winter-vivern-all-summer/). The APT, tracked as TA473, was reported by Recorded Future and Google’s TAG team *as being aligned with Belarusian and Russian government objectives* (https://www.avertium.com/resources/threat-reports/apt-winter-vivern-resurfaces). *In addition, US elected officials and staffers were also targeted* (https://www.proofpoint.com/us/blog/threat-insight/exploitation-dish-best-served-cold-winter-vivern-uses-known-zimbra-vulnerability). 

 Start date – End date: The campaign has been active since early 2021 and continues up to at least early 2023. 

 MITRE TTPs: ['T1071.001: Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols', 'T1566.001: Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment', 'T1105: Ingress Tool Transfer', 'T1218.013: Signed Binary Proxy Execution: Msiexec', 'T1059.001: Command and Scripting Interpreter: PowerShell'] 

 Impact: The impact involves unauthorized access to sensitive systems and information across multiple high-value government and private sector targets. 

 Mitigation: ['Implement multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all user accounts, especially those with privileged access.', 'Conduct regular phishing awareness training for employees, emphasizing the identification of phishing emails and suspicious websites.', 'Deploy and maintain up-to-date anti-virus and anti-malware solutions across all systems.', 'Regularly update and patch software to mitigate vulnerabilities that could be exploited by attackers.', 'Monitor network traffic for unusual activity and employ intrusion detection systems (IDS) to identify potential threats.', 'Implement strict access controls and regularly review user permissions to ensure the principle of least privilege is maintained.']
