#### Incident: DDoSia Malware Launching DDoS Attacks Against Korean Institutions 

#### Root cause 
 The root cause behind the incident is the use of automated DDoS bots like DDoSia by the Russian hacktivist group NoName057 (16), along with pro-Russian hacktivist groups Cyber Army of Russia Reborn and Alixsec, to launch DDoS attacks against targets with anti-Russian views. *The attacks were triggered by remarks made by Minister of Foreign Affairs Cho Tae-yul and President Yoon Suk-yeol regarding the supply of weapons to Ukraine* (https://asec.ahnlab.com/en/84531/). *The DDoSia project launched on Telegram in early 2022, with channels gaining over 10,000 users and facilitating cryptocurrency payments for participants* (https://blog.sekoia.io/following-noname05716-ddosia-projects-targets/). *Cyberoo IRT analyzed the latest version of the tool in December 2023, revealing AES-GCM encryption and enhanced authentication mechanisms* (https://cert.cyberoo.com/en/noname05716-ddosia-tool-analysis-report/). *NETSCOUT identified NoName057(16)'s reliance on HTTPS application-layer DDoS attacks, with attacks sourced from legitimate CDN and cloud networks* (https://www.netscout.com/blog/asert/noname057-16). 

#### Threat actor/group/campaign 
 NoName057 (16), Cyber Army of Russia Reborn, Alixsec 

#### Organization/industry/location 
 Major South Korean government agencies and organizations. *The group also targeted NATO countries (Lithuania, Poland, Czech Republic, Latvia), Western countries (France, United Kingdom, Italy, Canada), as well as specific domains such as id.kyivcity.gov.ua, zno.testportal.com.ua, e-journal.iea.gov.ua, wagnercentr.ru, wagner2022.ru, ratp.fr, ratp-m2e.fr, mutuelleratp.fr, cgt-ratp.fr, transfert-ratp.fr* (https://blog.sekoia.io/following-noname05716-ddosia-projects-targets/). 

#### Start date – End date

#### Impact 
 Various South Korean organizations suffered damages due to the DDoS attacks.
