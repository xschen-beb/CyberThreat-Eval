#### Incident: BianLian Ransomware Data Theft Focus 

#### Root cause 
 Misconfigured Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) services and exploited vulnerabilities in Windows and ESXi infrastructure, such as the ProxyShell exploit chain (CVE-2021-34473, CVE-2021-34523, CVE-2021-31207) and CVE-2022-37969 for privilege escalation on Windows 10 and 11. The use of stolen RDP credentials and custom Go-based backdoors facilitated unauthorized access. *The threat group has honed its tactics since its first appearance in July 2022* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bianlian-ransomware-gang-shifts-focus-to-pure-data-extortion/). *Use of commercial remote access tools has also been noted* (https://heimdalsecurity.com/blog/bianlian-ransomware-data-theft/). *BianLian actors use open-source tools and command-line scripting for discovery and credential harvesting* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa23-136a). *SOC Prime�s platform provides relevant Sigma rules for detection* (https://socprime.com/blog/bianlian-ransomware-detection/). *New tactics include using Ngrok and modified Rsocks for command and control* (https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/bianlian-ransomware-new-tactics/). *Updated advisory from U.S. and Australian authorities highlights evolving tactics* (https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/feds-warn-new-bianlian-ransomware-group-attack-profile-a-26888). 

#### Threat actor/group/campaign 
 BianLian ransomware and extortion group, primarily operating from Russia, uses foreign-language names for evasion. *Redacted, a cybersecurity company, has reported on their activities* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bianlian-ransomware-gang-shifts-focus-to-pure-data-extortion/). *Multiple affiliates based in Russia are involved* (https://heimdalsecurity.com/blog/bianlian-ransomware-data-theft/). *CISA, FBI, and ACSC have released a joint advisory on their activities* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa23-136a). *The group has been linked to Pioneer Kitten* (https://socprime.com/blog/bianlian-ransomware-detection/). *BianLian employs external proxy Rsocks* (https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/feds-warn-new-bianlian-ransomware-group-attack-profile-a-26888). 

#### Organization/industry/location 
 Various small to medium-sized organizations, including notable breaches at Air Canada, Northern Minerals, and Boston Children's Health Physicians. *BianLian has listed a total of 118 victim organizations, with 71% being U.S.-based companies* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bianlian-ransomware-gang-shifts-focus-to-pure-data-extortion/). *Northern Minerals disclosed a breach in late March 2024, with data published on the dark web* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/australian-mining-company-discloses-breach-after-bianlian-leaks-data/). *Boston Children's Health Physicians (BCHP) was compromised in September 2024, exposing data of employees, patients, and guarantors* (https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/bianlian-ransomware-claims-attack-on-boston-childrens-health-physicians/). *The group has also targeted U.S. and Australian critical infrastructure sectors* (https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa23-136a). *Targeting healthcare and manufacturing organizations noted by Grayson North from GuidePoint* (https://www.bankinfosecurity.com/feds-warn-new-bianlian-ransomware-group-attack-profile-a-26888).
