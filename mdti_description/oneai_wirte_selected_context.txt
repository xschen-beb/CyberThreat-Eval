Threat Actor: WIRTE
Context: WIRTE is a Middle Eastern cyber espionage group active since at least 2019. They primarily target government and diplomatic entities, especially in the Middle East and North Africa (MENA) region. WIRTE is known for using spear-phishing campaigns to deliver custom malware, such as the Zebrocy toolset, to infiltrate and exfiltrate sensitive information from their targets. They also employ other tactics like leveraging publicly available exploits and document-based malware to achieve their espionage objectives. WIRTE's activities often align with regional geopolitical interests, indicating possible state sponsorship or alignment with national goals.
